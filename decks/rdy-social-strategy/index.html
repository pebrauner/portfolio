2

Numbers Don't Lie

Engagement engine. Breaks the clip machine perception with data
IG FeedX
Concept

Carousel stat cards on IG Feed + punchy stat tweets with graphic on X. Uses the data and statistics rdy.gg already produces for the website. No static single images, only carousels (Instagram algorithm penalizes static posts).

Format: IG Carousel Structure

Slide 1: Provocative headline stat ("s1mple's rating in his last 10 maps: 1.42")
Slide 2: Context and comparison
Slide 3: Head-to-head or ranking visual
Slide 4: CTA: "Who's better? Drop it in the comments"

On X: One-liner + Bryan's stat graphic. Designed to get quote-tweeted.

Why It Works

Carousels get 1.4× wider reach and 3.1× higher engagement than static images. Saves and shares (the most valuable IG signals) are highest for carousels. The Filipino Dota community and Central Asian CS community are intensely opinionated about stats, and provocative stats drive comment wars, which is the #1 engagement signal.

Ownership

Arnoud (posting) · Bryan (carousel design system) · Pedro (stat selection)

Cadence

3× per week.

Example Carousel Stat Card 4-slide carousel mockup

Algorithm insight: Saves and shares are worth 3× more than likes. A single save = more than 50 generic likes on Facebook. Design every carousel to be either saveable (educational) or shareable (hot take).

Video & Podcast Pipeline

We already produce 3-5 original videos per week plus two active podcasts. This content is the foundation of our social presence and needs to be fully integrated into the campaign framework, not treated as a separate effort.

The Supercomputer Principle: Every video and podcast episode contains multiple standalone social moments. A 45-minute Win Condition episode might produce 3-5 clips that each work as independent content on social. The goal is to think of each clip not as "here is our podcast" but as "here is one insight from our work that stands on its own."

Research Appendix

Algorithm mechanics, platform benchmarks, and rdy.gg's own analytics. The data behind every decision in this document.

Instagram Algorithm 2025-2026

Top Ranking Signals

1. Watch Time. Viewers decide in 1.7 seconds. 60%+ retention is the target. The last 10 seconds matter as much as the hook: if people replay or linger at the end, the algorithm reads that as a strong signal.

2. Likes per Reach. The percentage of viewers who like, measured against how many people saw it, not total followers.

3. Sends per Reach. How often content is shared via DM. Third most powerful signal and the hardest to fake.

Hidden Engagement Weights

Saves and shares carry 3× the weight of likes. Dwell time, scroll velocity, and completion rate are all tracked passively. Meaningful comments (more than 4 words) carry more weight than emoji reactions. This is why carousels that teach something outperform highlights that entertain.

Posting Behaviour

The algorithm penalises accounts that delete and repost. Consistency of posting schedule matters more than posting frequency. Engaging with comments in the first 60 minutes after posting significantly boosts distribution.

Format Comparison

Reels: 30.81% avg reach rate. The discovery engine. Hook in first 3 seconds. 30-90 second sweet spot. Vertical 9:16 only.

Carousels: 14.45% reach but 10% engagement rate — 109% more engagement per person reached than Reels. Highest save rate of any format. Up to 20 slides; the algorithm re-serves carousels to people who didn't swipe the first time.

Static images: 7% engagement, 125% lower reach than Reels. Avoid for feed posts. Only acceptable use is Stories.

Optimal weekly mix: 3-4 Reels + 2-3 carousels. No static feed posts.

Stories

Stories do not affect feed algorithm directly, but consistent Story posting increases profile visits, which is a secondary feed ranking signal. Completion rate and poll/sticker interactions are the key Story metrics. Ideal length: 3-7 frames per Story set.

X / Twitter Algorithm 2025-2026

Feed Structure

X surfaces three feeds: For You (algorithm), Following (chronological), and Search. The For You tab is the discovery surface and the one that matters for growth. It operates on an interest graph, not a social graph, meaning a 140-follower account can reach the same audience as a 140K account if the content signals are right. The March 6 spike in rdy.gg's own analytics (357 sessions from X in a single day) is a direct example of this ceiling.

Key Ranking Signals

Replies (highest weight) → Reposts → Likes → Bookmarks → Profile clicks. Engagement velocity in the first 30 minutes is the most critical window. A post that gets 20 replies in 30 minutes will be distributed far more aggressively than one that gets 200 likes over 24 hours.

Content Quality

X Premium (verified) accounts get a distribution multiplier in For You. This is why getting X Premium for the CS2 account is a practical priority, not just an analytics unlock. Posts with external links receive reduced distribution; the workaround is to post the link in the first reply rather than the main post.

Format Performance

Video: Native video uploaded directly to X outperforms linked video by a significant margin. X's algorithm actively promotes native video in For You. Vertical video (9:16) performs best on mobile.

Threads: High engagement format for narrative content. Each reply in a thread gets its own distribution opportunity. Good fit for Deep Dive Drop and Cold Read long-form.

Images: 2-4 image posts outperform single images. Alt text improves accessibility and is indexed by search.

rdy.gg Benchmark

Dota X account: 6.6% engagement rate against an industry average of 0.5-2% for media accounts. X referral traffic to rdy.gg: 37.79% engaged session rate, 31s average time on site — the second highest quality traffic source after Organic Search.

Facebook Algorithm 2025-2026

The Recommendation Shift

Up to 50% of Feed is now recommended content from pages users do not follow. This is a TikTok-style interest graph model. A page with 753 followers has the same viral ceiling as a page with 5M followers if the content signals are strong enough. The implication: growing the page follower count matters less than producing content the algorithm wants to recommend.

Most Valuable Signals

Saves (highest weight) → Shares to Stories → Shares via DM → Long-form comments → Reactions. A single save is algorithmically worth more than 50 generic likes. Content that teaches, informs, or surprises tends to generate saves; content that entertains tends to generate reactions. Aim for the former.

Native Content vs Links

Facebook actively suppresses link posts in organic distribution. Native video, text posts, and image posts all outperform link-only posts significantly. The workaround: post the content natively (the clip, the stat image, the key quote), then add the article link in the first comment. This is the standard approach for media pages operating within the algorithm's preferences.

Groups as Organic Reach

Facebook Groups have 1.8B monthly active users and are treated as high-trust surfaces by the algorithm. Participating authentically in regional esports groups (SEA Dota communities, Turkish CS groups, Brazilian esports pages) builds presence without paid spend. Sharing value first, not links.
