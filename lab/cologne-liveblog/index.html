The Day IEM Cologne Major · Stage 1

On site for Stage 1 and it is a strange, quiet kind of pressure. No arena, no anthem, half the famous names still in their hotels waiting for next week. Just sixteen teams in a studio fighting for the right to matter at all.

SINNERS are out, and beastik sat down after the 0-2 to TYLOO and said he would almost rather not have qualified than go out like that. You could feel that one in the room. The grind down here is the most honest part of the whole Major, and the most brutal.
