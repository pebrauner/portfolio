Cosmic Hypothetical
A Magazine of Curiosities
Printed in browsers Independent / Irregular Sundays, mostly

A magazine
of fun facts.

Each weekend, I disappear into a question that has no business mattering. How loud can a sound be? How old is the d20 I rolled on Tuesday? How far is the largest black hole we've ever seen?

The answers turn out stranger than they should be. Cosmic Hypothetical is where I keep them — one issue at a time.

The premise is small. Take one number, one phenomenon, one curiosity — and follow it down until it stops being a fact and becomes a story. Sometimes that takes a paragraph. Sometimes it takes ten thousand years of cuneiform tablets, a Prussian artillery officer, and a pulp novelist in 1950.

There is no schedule. There is no brief. Each issue arrives when it arrives, illustrated and footnoted and probably too long. If you find yourself reading at 2 a.m. and arguing with the math, that is the correct response.
